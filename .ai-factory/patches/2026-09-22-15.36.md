# Restore the complete panel splitter hit area

## Cause and fix

The shell splitter already has z=61 above panel content and the docked viewer
(z=60). Its leading hit inset, however, excluded almost all of the left half
of the gutter to reserve an old scrollbar lane. Full-width panel renderers
therefore received those pointer events. Remove that leading exclusion so
both halves of the shell-level splitter own hover and drag input. Panel expand
buttons remain above it at z=62.

## Verification

Extended the existing splitter interaction test to hover and press both edges.
It failed at left-edge hover before the fix and passes afterward. Drag update
coalescing, double-click reset, panel expand controls, and docked viewer
interaction passed. The 175% test also checks physical-pixel geometry and unit
transforms for the splitter line/track and captures rendered output.
Static QML import verification passed; rebuilt the static host, embedded
payload, and worktree launcher.

## Prevention

When changing panel bounds, check both stacking order and excluded hit regions.
Testing only the center or right side of the splitter misses this regression.
