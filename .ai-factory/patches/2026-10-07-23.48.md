# Keep viewer-flight layout stable and restore pixel-aligned presentation

**Date:** 2026-10-07 23:48 MSK
**Files:** `third_party/ZoinGallery/qml/GalleryViewerSurface.qml`,
`third_party/ZoinGallery/qml/GalleryVideoPlaybackSurface.qml`,
`third_party/ZoinGallery/qml/ViewerImageLayer.qml`,
`third_party/ZoinGallery/tests/GalleryQmlInteractionTest.cpp`,
`qt/host/tests/F4QuickViewSurfaceTests.cpp`
**Severity:** medium

## Problem

Viewer opening/closing flights still felt uneven after selecting hardware
sampling during animation. Native profiling found repeated viewport resizing
and refitting on each animation tick. The composed video overlay also exposed
a half-physical-pixel resting origin at DPR 1.75.

## Root Cause

The flight animated the inner FlickableZoomable width and height. Both setters
triggered resizeViewport()/zoomToFit(), recomputed image placement, and propagated
layout/binding changes. In a fresh five-close/five-open baseline, every flight
ran ten width and ten height handlers despite presenting the same media.

Changing only to a uniform scene transform is insufficient: stableCenter()
still snapped the image center during motion, introducing up to half a physical
pixel of discontinuity. Separately, mapToItem() is not a notifying dependency on
ancestor geometry or a Translate transform. A video play-button origin stayed
at 652.5 physical pixels even when its wrapper appeared aligned.

The earlier driver allocation/reclaim stall is a separate, unresolved cause.
Do not infer its exact resource owner from temporal correlation with a flight.

## Solution

- Keep the inner viewport at the final viewer size. Fit the media into the
  interpolated source/target rectangle with one uniform scale and translation;
  preserve aspect ratio and return the viewport transform to identity at rest.
- Keep the spherical presentation inside that transform, but move native video
  text/icons to an unscaled, final-size overlay. Fade it with flight progress.
- Include ancestor geometry and the host's pixelAlignmentRevision in overlay
  alignment dependencies before mapping and rounding the final scene origin.
- Suppress stable-center snapping only during hardwareSampling. An initial
  broader change broke wheelMinificationKeepsItsCenterDuringAnimation; narrowing
  the condition restored that check without giving up smooth flight geometry.
- Add opt-in boundary diagnostics with `[FIX:viewer-flight]`, enabled by the
  existing `--debug-viewer-transition` argument. No per-frame logging is added.
- Add behavior regressions for landscape, portrait/wide tile, quarter rotation,
  odd source dimensions, transition reversal, final transforms, and actual
  composed text/raster-icon leaves at 175%. On Linux OpenGL, request a core
  context for the test fixture so its baked GLSL 150 shader is supported.

No shader-quality change, decoder change, dependency rebuild, unbounded cache,
or speculative resource-retention change is part of this fix. Existing dirty
audio, video-cache, and hardware-sampling work is preserved.

### Resource-lifetime limit

A separate sampled resource capture observed three QGles2Texture::create calls
and two QGles2Buffer::create calls around a playing close. It captured one
complete close/open pair despite three scripted attempts and visibly perturbed
timing, so its frame gaps are not comparative benchmark evidence. Qt/Mesa stack
unwinding was incomplete, and no linear-video-texture release or reclaim stack
was captured. Absence of a sampled stack does not prove absence of that work.

Switching between retained image-pyramid and direct video-plane material is a
source-level candidate for boundary allocations, not proven ownership of the
specific stalled Mesa allocation. The exact resource behind the earlier driver
allocation/reclaim delay remains unidentified. Retaining a large float texture
or raising cache budgets without that proof would be speculative.

## Prevention

- Prefer a presentation transform to animating viewport dimensions when the
  inner content has fit/crop/layout handlers. Test the mapped media bounds,
  aspect ratio, reversal, and identity at rest rather than only the container.
- At fractional DPR, inspect every actual native text/raster-icon leaf through
  the complete ancestor chain. Assert unit basis vectors as well as origins;
  an aligned wrapper does not cover its children.
- Treat mapToItem-based alignment as needing explicit geometry/transform
  dependencies. Include externally mutated Translate lists through a revision.
- Keep animation-only continuous geometry separate from wheel-zoom centering
  and static pixel snapping; run the neighboring wheel test before accepting
  a generalized condition.
- Verify shader captures contain real content, use a supported OpenGL context,
  and keep hardware render-control tests separate from unreliable platform
  window readback. Never turn a blank or garbled grab into visual proof.
- Measure first-frame and endpoint delay separately from interior cadence. Keep
  cold/warm and paused/playing samples distinct, and report probe overhead and
  incomplete stack unwinding. Do not increase memory retention based only on a
  driver-stall correlation.
