# Fractional Wayland viewer jumped to the wrong settled geometry

**Date:** 2026-09-19
**Files:** qt/host/qml/ShellSurfaceHost.qml; third_party/ZoinGallery/ViewerResampleEffect.cpp; third_party/ZoinGallery/resources/viewer_resample.vert; third_party/ZoinGallery/tests/ViewerResampleGpuTest.cpp
**Severity:** high

## Problem

On a 150% Wayland desktop, image motion rendered at the correct scale and offset, but Fit and 100% jumped to a larger, displaced rectangle as soon as the animation settled. The same image behaved correctly on macOS and Windows.

## Root Cause

The embedded viewer received `QScreen::devicePixelRatio`, which is the integer Wayland output scale (2) on this compositor. The concrete `QQuickWindow` and its OpenGL render target use the fractional DPR (1.5). Animated frames preserved Qt's render-target-aware matrix, while the settled resampling vertex shader replaced that projection with an extent derived from the incorrect screen DPR. The mismatch therefore affected only resting frames.

## Solution

Bind the viewer to `QQuickWindow::devicePixelRatio`, which exposes the effective render-target DPR. Keep the settled pixel-grid correction only when its requested physical extent agrees with the scale projected by Qt's matrix; otherwise retain Qt's projection and continuous filtering. Add an environment-gated `[FIX:viewer-resample-dpr]` diagnostic for the render target, requested extent and alignment state.

## Prevention

The GPU regression models a screen DPR of 2 with a window/render-target DPR of 1.5 and compares both moving and settled frames. It failed before the fix and now passes on hardware OpenGL (Mesa Apple M2). Existing 175% settled-frame and animation-restoration checks pass, the embedded viewer's 175% host test passes, and live Fit/100% verification of `work/f4-final-wayland.png` reports 92% Fit and an exact 2560x1600 100% extent with target DPR 1.5. The portable Qt host and static Go launcher audits pass.
