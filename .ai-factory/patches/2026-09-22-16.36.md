# Group header pixel alignment and noninteractive top spacing

## Reproduced

- At 175% DPR, moving an ancestor after header creation left the title at
  physical X=14.350000. The separator also retained a stale position
  (physical Y=287.525000) during the initial partial correction.
- Clicking the top spacing incorrectly collapsed the group.

## Changes

- Track ancestor geometry once on the shared header layer and snap header
  positions in scene space. Derive separator geometry from reactive positions.
- Explicitly align native title/count text and the raster chevron; retain unit
  transforms and the chevron immediately after the title.
- Exclude the top gap from direct and shared group hit testing and restrict
  hover painting to the header body.
- Add regressions for ancestor movement, every visual leaf's scene origin and
  unit transform, loaded SVG rendering, and gap/body clicks.

## Verification

- Focused header/pointer tests: 6 passed at 175% DPR.
- All five MasonryVisualDpr CTest variants passed (100/125/150/175/200%).
- Inspected the rendered 175% capture, including the small count and chevron.
- Rebuilt the static Qt host; qmlImportsWithoutInstalledQt passed.
- Regenerated the embedded payload and rebuilt the CGO-disabled worktree
  executable. Launched directly with --gui=qt using a fresh extraction cache.
- Parent repository and Gallery git diff --check passed.

Existing unrelated changes were preserved. No commit or push.
