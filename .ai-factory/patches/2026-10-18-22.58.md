# Reveal clipped workspace tabs before synthetic clicks

**Date:** 2026-10-18 22:58
**Files:** qt/host/tests/F4OperationsQueueTests.cpp
**Severity:** low

## Problem

`searchMessageStaysInDocumentAndOnlyBlocksTab` failed at its Commander activation
assertion under the offscreen platform at both 100% and 175% scale.

## Root Cause

The 1024-pixel fixture window leaves a narrow workspace-tab viewport beside the
title-bar controls. Its horizontal Flickable initially shows the trailing tab.
Commander remains `isVisible()` but is clipped out; at 175% its scene-space
center was (-101, 24). The synthetic mouse click therefore missed the window.
This was a test setup defect, not an application input-routing defect.

## Solution

Reveal the leading tab by resetting its ancestor Flickable's contentX. Assert
that the click point lies inside both the tab viewport and the window. Retain
the native mouse click and the exact workspace.activate/commander-tab assertion
for both editor and viewer, together with all existing dialog geometry checks.
Optional F4_TRACE_TEST_INPUT diagnostics use [FIX:search-tab].

## Verification

Before: `env QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen QT_SCALE_FACTOR=1.75
qt/host/build-qt-run2-video-artifactory/F4OperationsQueueTests
searchMessageStaysInDocumentAndOnlyBlocksTab` failed at line 925. Scale 1 also
failed. After rebuilding only F4OperationsQueueTests, both commands pass
(3 passed, 0 failed each). Related workspace retention, dialog move/resize and
keyboard-focus tests pass at 175% (6 passed including init/cleanup).

## Prevention

QQuickItem::isVisible does not prove that a point survives ancestor clipping.
Reveal scrollable targets and verify viewport containment before synthetic input.
Additional useful coverage would exercise switching tabs through a modal dialog
after an actual horizontal pointer gesture in a narrow title bar.
