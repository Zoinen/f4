# Keep group header content clear of the scrollbar

## Cause and solution

Group header labels/counts used a fixed right margin, independent of the
scrollbar's lane. Expose the visible vertical scrollbar's actual inset through
panel chrome and overlays; reserve that inset plus four pixels in the header
content. Keep the separator stroke full-width. Columns and panels without a
vertical scrollbar retain their normal content margin.

## Verification

The grouped overflow regression failed before implementation because the count
extended into the scrollbar area. It now checks count right edges, scene-space
origins and unit transforms at DPR 1.75 and captures rendered output. Ten
Gallery results passed, including all group-header pixel-grid cases, grouped
Off transitions, and scrollbar dragging. Static QML imports passed. Rebuilt
the static host, embedded payload and portable worktree launcher.

## Prevention

Derive header clearance from the actual interactive overlay bounds rather than
duplicating its position arithmetic or relying on a fixed margin.
