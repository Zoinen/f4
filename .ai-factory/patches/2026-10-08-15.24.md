# Scroll clipped video labels into view before pixel captures

**Date:** 2026-10-08 15:23 MSK
**Files:** `qt/host/tests/F4QuickViewSurfaceTests.cpp`
**Severity:** medium

## Problem

The ARM64 portable release CTest run still failed in
`F4GalleryVideoSettingsPixelGridTest`. The test's new viewport-height
assumption failed: `galleryViewport->height()` remained smaller than the
Gallery page's `implicitHeight()` even after enlarging the offscreen window.

## Root Cause

The settings dialog is constrained by the available screen geometry, so
resizing its test window cannot guarantee that a long settings page fits in a
clipped `Flickable`. Its full and Quick View video labels occupy different
scroll positions and must be rendered in separate captures.

## Solution

Keep the existing full-page scene-coordinate/transform checks, capture the full
video labels at the top of the viewport, then scroll the Quick View labels into
the viewport and capture/verify their rendered glyphs independently. Each
capture asserts that its text leaves are inside the clipped viewport.

## Prevention

- Do not assume an offscreen window resize can exceed the platform's available
  screen geometry.
- For pixel checks in clipped scrolling content, position each target leaf in
  the viewport and verify clipping geometry before inspecting its pixels.
- Keep scene-space physical-pixel and unit-transform assertions over the full
  page, independent of which portion is visible in an individual screenshot.
