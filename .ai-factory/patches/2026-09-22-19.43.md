# Center the scrollbar in the panel's tile gutter

## Root cause

The fixed eight-pixel right inset placed the thumb too far left, over the
rightmost tile edge. The lane is defined by content padding plus half the
inter-tile spacing, rather than by an independent scrollbar margin.

## Solution

Center the scrollbar in that computed lane, keeping its hit rectangle inside
the panel for narrower/default lanes. Round the final scene-space X with the
attached Screen DPR, including ancestor offsets. Do not use the layout's
optional DPR override as proof of the actual window DPR.

## Verification

The embedded-padding centering regression failed before implementation.
An intermediate change exposed a physical handle origin of 1097.25 at DPR
1.75; the corrected origin is 1097. Track/handle origins and unit transforms,
rendered capture, hover/drag, grouped overflow and disabling, sparse scrolling,
splitter interaction and static QML imports passed. Six Gallery and four host
test results passed, including setup/cleanup. Rebuilt the portable launcher.

## Prevention

Derive overlay placement from the surrounding content geometry. Check actual
visual leaves at fractional DPR and preserve adjacent-control input ownership.
