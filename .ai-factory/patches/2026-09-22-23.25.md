# Center compact-view portrait thumbnails

**Date:** 2026-09-22 23:25
**Files:** `third_party/ZoinGallery/qml/GalleryThumbnailCompactLayer.qml`, `third_party/ZoinGallery/tests/GalleryQmlInteractionTest.cpp`
**Severity:** UI regression

## Problem

Portrait thumbnails in Details and Columns views were aligned to the left edge of their fixed preview slots, while the intended placement was centered.

## Root Cause

The compact thumbnail image retained `x: 0` after its width became aspect-aware and narrower than the square slot for portrait images.

## Solution

Center the image within the stable slot using device-pixel-rounded coordinates. Keep filename positioning tied to the slot, so text alignment remains identical with and without thumbnails. Add a regression assertion for the image center at DPR 1.75. The test failed before the change and passed afterward; the rebuilt running app was visually checked.

## Prevention

When changing thumbnail sizing, assert both image placement within the preview slot and the separate slot-to-text geometry across compact modes and thumbnail states.
