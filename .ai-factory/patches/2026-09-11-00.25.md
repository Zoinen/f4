**Severity:** medium

## Problem

Qt Settings omitted custom radio choices and pointer explanations. Lists lacked
the input background and persistent overflow scrollbars. Disabled inputs looked
and behaved like enabled ones.

## Root Cause

Settings radios inherited Group semantics, but their individual buttons were not
semantic providers. Hover had no owner-side event. Dropdown pointer selection
bypassed OnSelect and declared a menu-only update. Native field wrappers did not
bind disabled state, and list views used a fading or absent scrollbar. A terminal
single-row radio set also allocated only 7.33 DIP per choice in the old Qt renderer.

## Solution

Publish full radio labels, independent selection/focus and disabled choice indices.
Route control.explain to the Settings owner without focus or scroll changes; both
captions and controls identify their help owner. Notify dropdown owners before a
full redraw when the highlighted choice changes. Native hover preserves Qt focus.
Measure and render radio choices with one QML component at their available width,
including multiline captions and focus frames. Apply disabled input styling and
input-colored list surfaces, and display scrollbars for as long as content overflows.
Snap scrollbar fill extent and final scene origin.

## Prevention

- Reproduce missing semantics and owner notifications independently of Show().
- Exercise disabled controls and choices, full source labels, selection vs focus,
  unchanged hover, native focus preservation and typed protocol roundtrips.
- Check overflow both before and after replacing a list with short content.
- Measure using the same component, font and available width as visible controls.
  Resizing to 520 DIP reproduced a following-row overlap before the width fix.
- Check actual leaf scene origins and unit transforms at DPR 1.75, after scroll
  and resize, and inspect rendered small text. The persistent scrollbar regression
  measured its fill at y=348.105 physical pixels before correction; its physical
  height was fractional too. Wrapper-only checks would have missed it.
- Use the shared test/production QML manifest and verify the packaged static host
  starts before replacing a working worktree build.
