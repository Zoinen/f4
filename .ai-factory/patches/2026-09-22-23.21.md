# Portrait thumbnails right-aligned in fixed gallery slots

**Date:** 2026-09-22 23:21
**Files:** `third_party/ZoinGallery/qml/GalleryThumbnailCompactLayer.qml`, `third_party/ZoinGallery/tests/GalleryQmlInteractionTest.cpp`
**Severity:** medium

## Problem

Portrait thumbnails in enlarged Details and Columns rows were aligned to the right edge of their square preview slots, unlike the left-aligned icons and thumbnails expected by the user.

## Root Cause

The preceding alignment fix correctly kept one common slot and text origin, but used `x: parent.width - width` for the aspect-ratio-sized image. That traded stable filename alignment for inconsistent thumbnail alignment.

## Solution

Placed the portrait image at `x: 0` inside its unchanged square slot. A DPR 1.75 regression check first found its left edge 106 logical pixels away from the slot's left edge; after the fix the two coincide in Details and both column modes. The text-origin alignment and fixed slot-to-text gutter checks still pass. The complete GalleryQmlInteractionTest passed, and the rebuilt Qt host was visually checked in f4.

## Prevention

Test preview alignment independently of filename alignment. A portrait fitted without cropping cannot simultaneously fill a square slot and have no blank area before a fixed text column; avoid claiming both goals can be met by moving the thumbnail. Additional useful coverage is a landscape/portrait/missing-preview visual comparison at several zoom values.
