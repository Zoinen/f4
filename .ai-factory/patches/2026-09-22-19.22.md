# Respect video decoder capability and reserve the scrollbar lane

**Date:** 2026-09-22 19:22
**Severity:** medium

## Problem

Video entries received 16:9 thumbnail geometry in a build without video decoding.
The vertical scrollbar extended outside the full-width panel, while the shell
splitter intercepted input on the left panel edge.

## Root Cause

External catalog ingestion trusted video hints without checking the compiled
decoder capability. Dense reset, sparse rows, and metadata updates each applied
the placeholder. The scrollbar retained an old +8 external offset after panel
padding moved inside. The splitter's leading exclusion had also been removed.

## Solution

Gate video image classification and placeholder dimensions on the existing
zoinVideoThumbnailsEnabled capability at every ingestion path, preserving video
identity and ordinary file-icon behavior when unavailable. Move the scrollbar
16 logical pixels left, to an eight-pixel internal inset. Reserve the left
splitter half for panel controls, retaining the divider line itself in its hit
area with fractional-DPR-aware rounding.

Fresh Gallery test linkage also needed the static QuickEffects backing library,
matching the existing host-test link contract.

## Verification and prevention

Regression-first checks reproduced unsupported video dimensions, the external
scrollbar edge (648 instead of 632), and a splitter drag from the panel lane.
Dense/sparse/metadata capability tests now pass. The full Gallery session suite
passed 60 tests with two skips. Scrollbar geometry, hover, drag, actual handle
and track scene origins/unit transforms, and rendered capture passed at DPR
1.75. Splitter hover/drag, expand controls and static-only QML imports passed.
Keep capability checks in catalog ingestion, not only decoder dispatch; test
both adjacent input lanes whenever changing panel padding or splitter bounds.
