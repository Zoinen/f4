# Fix review: multiline navigation and word dragging

## Observed regression

The panel hotkey layer intercepted text-navigation keys before the focused command line. Double-click word selection in the command line, editor, and terminal output did not retain word granularity during the ensuing mouse drag. The command-line scrollbar omitted its text insets from its extent.

## Cause and change

- Route focused command-line navigation before panel/plugin key handling, preserving Shift selection and page movement through the multiline edit.
- Preserve initial word bounds across drag updates in each selection owner.
- Derive scrollbar size, position, and maximum offset from the same complete content extent.

## Verification and future guardrail

Go regression tests cover Search First navigation and console/editor word dragging. Qt surface tests cover command-line selection, scrollbar endpoints, and terminal word dragging. Keep pointer gesture state in the selection owner, and assert both directions and release in future tests.
