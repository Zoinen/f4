chevron against the right-aligned count. Size the title to its measured text
width, bounded by the space reserved for the chevron and count, and anchor the
chevron six logical pixels after it. Long titles still elide.

Reserve eight additional logical pixels in native section geometry above the
separator. Offset the separator and content together so the existing 28-pixel
content area does not shrink and following rows do not overlap. Pixel-snap the
spacing and title width; preserve the full-panel separator width.
